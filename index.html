Use Case — CPG Trade Promotion ROI Analysis

CPG manufacturers spend 15–25% of revenue on trade promotions with limited visibility into which are profitable. Manual ROI calculation requires analyst hours. This agent provides instant answers from a 2027 promotion plan (42 Trade Promotions, 25 products) and 2025–2026 syndicated sell-out actuals.

Key finding from dataset: 39 of 42 planned 2027 promotions have GP ROI < 1.0× — a signal that normally surfaces in weeks through traditional processes.
